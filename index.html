DO MORE • IGNITE YOUR SOCIAL LIFE

WHAT WE DO MORE THAN JUST A NIGHT OUT

Adventure

From axe throwing and motorsport to escape rooms and archery, no two meetings are the same. We do things that are genuinely fun, push you out of your comfort zone and leave you with a story worth telling.

Friendship

The best friendships form when you're doing something daft together on a Thursday night. Round Table is a group of lads from all walks of life, different jobs, different backgrounds, same sense of humour.

Community

We run Santa's Sleigh every Christmas, raise money for local causes and show up where it matters. Being part of Round Table means what you do has an impact beyond just a good night out.

SANTA'S SLEIGH

You've seen the sleigh. Now meet the club behind it. Every December we take to the streets of Lancaster, bring thousands of people together and raise money for the local causes that need it most — all powered by a group of lads who just want to make Christmas a little more magical.
